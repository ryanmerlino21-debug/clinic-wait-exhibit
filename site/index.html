Clinic Wait-Time Simulator

How much do staffing and queue length change how long patients wait? Move the sliders and a small neural network predicts the wait. The clinic is simulated, so no real patients are involved.

2. Check the model on visits it never saw

Each dot is one of 300 held-out visits. A dot on the diagonal line means the model got it exactly right. Click a dot to see that visit.

3. Ask the exhibit

A small language model runs in your browser (no server, nothing is sent anywhere) and answers from a short guide about this exhibit. It needs WebGPU and downloads about 0.7 GB the first time.

Data: clinic-wait-times.csv from the Neural Network Explorer course site. It is synthetic (made up), 2,000 visits.

Limits: this models a simulation, not a real clinic. Don't use it to plan real staffing. Chatbot answers can be wrong, so check them against this page and the guide.

Source and training script are on GitHub.
